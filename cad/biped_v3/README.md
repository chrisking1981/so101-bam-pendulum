# Biped version 3: eight servos, with knees (sketch)

Per leg: hip roll, hip pitch, knee, ankle pitch (8 × STS3215 12 V). Torso, hip cross bracket and foot as in
[version 2](../biped_v2/README.md); new are the **thigh** (80 mm, holds the hip-pitch servo upside down at the top and the knee servo
at the bottom) and the **shank** (60 mm, clevis on both knee horns, holds the ankle servo). About 240 mm tall.
Generated by [`../biped_v3.py`](../biped_v3.py); 3D model with joint sliders and animations: [viewer/](viewer/index.html).

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Official STS3215 STEP in every holder and clevis | no overlap |
| Joint sweep ([`../biped_v3_motion_check.py`](../biped_v3_motion_check.py)): roll ±30°, hip pitch ±60°, knee −10…90°, ankle ±45° | no collisions |
| Knee at 110° | thigh and shank touch (51 mm³): knee range 0–90° |

Not trained and not printed yet: this is a sketch to see how a knee would look. It needs two more 12 V servos.

## Electronics on top (2026-11-25)

[`../biped_v3_electronics.py`](../biped_v3_electronics.py). Hip pitch = C044 (5 V adapter), hip roll, knee and ankle = 12 V.

- **Board rack** (`rack.stl`, part of the print): screwed on the four M3 holes of the torso deck on 12 mm pillars, so the Nano stays
  underneath. Two square dovetail sockets take the official **Seeedstudio_Mounting_Plate_SO101** (in `cad/vendor/`, from the SO-ARM100
  repo), sliding in from the back as on the SO-101 base.
- **Boards:** Seeed bus servo driver board (XIAO socket, CH343 USB, 42 x 25 mm, M2 holes 16.4 x 21 mm = the plate's pattern), one per
  supply: 12 V on the left plate, 5 V on the right. USB-C points outward. Each board has **one** servo socket, so a small 1 -> 2
  splitter (bus hub) behind the rack feeds both legs.
- **Cables:** 12 V: splitter -> hip roll -> knee -> ankle per leg (daisy chain); 5 V: splitter -> each C044 hip pitch. The 12 V and
  5 V servos never share a cable. Sockets on the servos from the official STEP: back face, S.x = -1.6, S.y = +-5.2.
- **Cable clips** (moulded on the torso back, the thigh and the shank, inner side): a ring for a ~3 mm servo cable with a 2.6 mm
  mouth, so the cable clicks in.
- Masses for the simulation: rack 19 g and plates 14 g from the meshes, boards 2 x 10 g, splitters 2 x 3 g and cables 10 g estimated:
  **694 g** in total, centre of mass 134 mm high (626 g / 120 mm without).

The 3D viewer bends the cables with the joints. Connector places on the board and the splitter are approximate (from a photo).
