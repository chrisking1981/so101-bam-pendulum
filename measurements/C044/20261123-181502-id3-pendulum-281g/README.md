# C044 unit 1 (ID 3) with the 281 g pendulum

This is the same pendulum as the [C046 281 g set](../../C046/20261123-171452-id5-pendulum-281g/README.md): the short arm with the two large steel brackets
bolted through the 75 mm and 100 mm holes, 281 g in total, centre of mass at 92 mm. It was rebuilt from the 450 g configuration by removing the two small
brackets; the operator confirmed the large brackets were in the same holes as in the first 281 g set.

Settings:

- Supply: RD6006 at 7.40 V.
- P = 4/8/16/32, D = 0, profile off in EEPROM.
- Zero: hanging rest position raw 2047.
- BAM log values: `mass = 0.281`, `arm_mass = 0`, `length = 0.092`.
- 16 runs, no bus timeouts. The servo read 26 °C afterwards.

With these runs, the C044 unit 1 has the same three load configurations as the C046: no load, 281 g and 450 g. Together they make up the C044 fit set.
